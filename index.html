Periods

Distances in nm, speeds in kn. Made good is the straight line from the start to the end of the period. Efficiency is made good divided by sailed. Both, and mean COG, are blank when the boat sailed in very different directions.

Turns

How to use

  • Hover the track or the charts to read time, speed and course at that moment.
  • Drag across a chart to select a period. The map highlights it, Periods adds a Selection column, and Play replays only that period. Double-click a chart to reset.
  • Pin a selection to keep it as a column, for example the outbound and return legs. Click a pinned column's heading to select that period again.
  • Click a row in Turns, or a white dot on the track, to zoom to that turn.
