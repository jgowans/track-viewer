Track viewer
Open a GPX file, or drop one on the page.

Mark

Range and VMC are measured from this point. Drag the ⚑ on the map, pick a point, or type a position.

Legs

Distances in nm, speeds in kn. Made good is distance away from the mark until the turn-around, then back toward it. Efficiency is made good divided by sailed.

Turns

How to use

  • Hover the track or the charts to read time, speed and course at that moment.
  • Drag across a chart to select a period. The map highlights it, Legs adds a Selection column, and Play replays only that period. Double-click a chart to reset.
  • Click a row in Turns, or a white dot on the track, to zoom to that turn.
